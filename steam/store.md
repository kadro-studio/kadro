### About this software

```
[h2]A full video editor, free for good[/h2]
Kadro is a free, open-source video editor for game clips, YouTube videos, TikTok, Reels and Shorts. No watermarks, no subscriptions, no accounts, no trial. The interface is in English, Ukrainian and Russian.

[h2]Montage to the beat[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Auto montage:[/b] pick a song and your clips, and Kadro cuts them on the beat with transitions and a zoom punch on every cut.
[*][b]Dynamics:[/b] speed ramps (montage, hero, bullet, jump cut, flash in and out), camera moves (zoom, pan, handheld, pulse, tilt), a punch, shake, swing or blink on every beat, zoom cuts for talking videos and boomerangs.
[*][b]Templates:[/b] a finished edit with numbered slots — drop in your clips and the effects, transitions, titles and music timing stay in place.
[/list]

[h2]A real timeline[/h2]
[list]
[*]Multi-track editing with ripple, roll, slip and slide edits, snapping, markers and in/out points.
[*]A video's picture and sound on linked tracks, like in Vegas; fades on any clip with five curve shapes.
[*]Keyframes with smooth easing, speed from 0.1× to 16× without pitch change, freeze frames, reverse, cutting out pauses in speech.
[*]Simple mode and a guided tour for beginners; every professional tool when you want it.
[/list]

[h2]Effects, colour and text[/h2]
[list]
[*]26 shader effects, 19 shader and 22 classic transitions, 18 in and out animations, 17 animated elements (light leaks, film grain, bokeh, snow, VHS…).
[*]Colour wheels, RGB curves, selective colour, 3D LUTs and 15 film looks; blend modes, chroma key, masks, stabilisation, video scopes.
[*]22 title styles and 24 fonts with Cyrillic, plus your own fonts.
[/list]

[h2]Sound[/h2]
Volume keyframes, EQ, compressor, noise reduction, loudness normalisation, music ducking under speech and voice-over recording.

[h2]AI that stays on your PC[/h2]
Automatic subtitles and background removal for video and pictures run on your own computer. Your footage is never uploaded anywhere.

[h2]Community workshop[/h2]
Effects, transitions, filters, LUTs, title styles, themes and project templates from other users install in one click, and you can publish your own straight from Kadro. Write your own GLSL effects or bring a shader over from Shadertoy.

[h2]Export[/h2]
MP4 (H.264 / H.265), WebM, ProRes, GIF, MP3 and WAV, with NVENC, Quick Sync and AMF hardware encoding and ready presets for YouTube, TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Preview and export are drawn by the same engine, so you get exactly what you see.

[h2]Open source[/h2]
Kadro is MIT-licensed and its source code is public. It lives on voluntary support.
```

## Системные требования (Windows)

| | Минимальные | Рекомендуемые |
|---|---|---|
| ОС | Windows 10 64-bit | Windows 11 64-bit |
| Процессор | 64-битный, 4 потока | 6 ядер и больше |
| Память | 8 GB | 16 GB (для 4K) |
| Видеокарта | с поддержкой WebGL 2 / DirectX 11 | с аппаратным кодированием (NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF) |
| Место на диске | 1 GB | 5 GB (модели ИИ, лёгкие копии тяжёлых видео) |
| Сеть | — | для библиотеки, мастерской и первой загрузки моделей ИИ |

Программа занимает около 550 MB; модели ИИ (40–250 MB каждая) скачиваются при первом использовании функции.

## Анкета о содержимом (Content Survey)

- Насилия, сексуального содержимого и т. п. нет. Пользовательский контент: да — мастерская (модерируется, есть жалобы) и онлайн-библиотека (свободные лицензии).
- **Генеративный ИИ** (раздел 3). Черновик ответа — проверьте, что он правдив, и дополните:
  - *Live-Generated:* «Optional, local features: speech-to-text subtitles (Whisper) from the user's own audio and background removal (MODNet) from the user's own video. Both models run on the user's computer; nothing is sent anywhere. They do not generate new images, sounds or text from prompts; the user sees and edits every result before export.»
  - *Pre-Generated:* если в программе есть картинки, звуки, тексты или переводы, сделанные с помощью ИИ, — перечислите их. Раздел говорит о содержимом, которое получает пользователь; пишут ли в нём об ИИ-помощниках при написании кода — в документации Steam не сказано, при сомнении спросите поддержку Steam (Steamworks → Support).
